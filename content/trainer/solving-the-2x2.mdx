The **2x2x2 Pocket Cube** looks deceptively simple compared to a standard 3x3. With only eight corner pieces and no edges or fixed centers, it is the ideal puzzle to expand your twisty puzzle repertoire.

Because every piece on a 2x2 is a corner, you already possess nearly all the necessary fingertricks and algorithms from your 3x3 training.

```
[ 3x3 Cube ]               [ 2x2 Pocket Cube ]
• 8 Corners                 • 8 Corners ONLY
• 12 Edges                  • 0 Edges
• 6 Fixed Centers           • 0 Fixed Centers
```

> [!NOTE]
> Since the 2x2 has no fixed center pieces to define face colors, you choose your own starting color orientation. By standard convention, start by solving the **White face** on the bottom layer.

---

## 1. The 3-Step Solving Roadmap

We solve the 2x2 using a streamlined, beginner-friendly method:

```
[ Step 1: Solve First Layer (White) ]
Match White stickers and align all four side colors.
                 ↓
[ Step 2: Orient Last Layer (Yellow Face) ]
Use Sune or Anti-Sune to point all Yellow stickers upward.
                 ↓
[ Step 3: Permute Last Layer (PBL) ]
Swap adjacent or diagonal corners to complete the puzzle!
```

---

## 2. Step 1: Solving the First Layer

Pick any corner with a White sticker and place it in the bottom-front-right position with White facing down.

To insert the remaining three White corners:
1. Locate a White corner in the top layer.
2. Rotate the top layer (`U`) until that corner sits directly above its matching slot.
3. Repeat the familiar **Sexy Move** (`R U R' U'`) until the corner drops into place with White pointing down and its side colors matching adjacent pieces.

```notation
R U R' U'
```

## 3. Step 2: Orienting the Last Layer (OLL)

With the first layer solved and facing down (`D`), the top face (`U`) will contain between 0 and 4 Yellow stickers pointing up.

Turn the cube so you are looking at the Yellow face. Use the classic **Sune** algorithm to orient the corners:

```notation
R U R' U R U2 R'
```

### Recognition Patterns:
- **1 Yellow Corner Oriented (Fish / Sune)**: Place the solved yellow corner in the front-left position and execute Sune.
- **2 Yellow Corners Oriented**: Rotate `U` until headlights (two yellow stickers facing the same direction) face the left or front, then execute Sune.
- **0 Yellow Corners Oriented**: Execute Sune once to produce a 1-corner Sune state, position the solved corner in the front-left, and execute Sune again.

---

## 4. Step 3: Permuting the Last Layer (PBL)

Once the entire top face is Yellow, inspect the side colors of the top layer. There are only two possible situations:

### Case A: Adjacent Corner Swap (Headlights Found)
If two adjacent corners have matching side colors, you have **Headlights**.
1. Rotate `U` to align the headlights with their matching first-layer color on the **Back face (`B`)**.
2. Execute the classic **T-Permutation**:

```notation
R U R' U' R' F R2 U' R' U' R U R' F'
```

### Case B: Diagonal Corner Swap (No Headlights)
If no two adjacent corners match on the top layer, you must swap diagonally opposite corners using the **Y-Permutation**:

```notation
F R U' R' U' R U R' F' R U R' U' R' F R F'
```

## 7. Key Takeaways

- **Corners Only**: The 2x2 consists entirely of 8 corner pieces.
- **Relative Positioning**: Build the first layer using adjacent color matching rather than fixed centers.
- **Algorithm Reuse**: Sune (`R U R' U R U2 R'`), T-Perm, and Y-Perm translate directly from your 3x3 knowledge.
- **Fast Execution**: A beginner 2x2 solve requires only 3 distinct stages: First Layer, OLL, and PBL.
