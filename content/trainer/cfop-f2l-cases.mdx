When speedcubers talk about "Full F2L," they are referring to a catalog of **41 possible piece arrangements** for a single corner-edge slot.

Looking at 41 separate algorithm formulas can seem overwhelming. But here is the secret that every advanced speedcuber knows: **you do not need to memorize 41 algorithms.**

Every single F2L case reduces to **three fundamental archetypes** based on where the pieces are located and which colors are facing up. Once you understand the geometric logic of these three archetypes, you can solve all 41 cases intuitively!

> [!NOTE]
> In CFOP, we always use an **unsolved slot** to hide a piece temporarily, reposition the matching piece on the top layer (`U`), join them into a 2x1 block, and insert the pair.

## 1. The 3 Fundamental F2L Case Archetypes

```
                          [ All 41 F2L Cases ]
                                   │
     ┌─────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────┐
     ▼                             ▼                             ▼
[ Archetype 1 ]             [ Archetype 2 ]             [ Archetype 3 ]
Free / Connected Pairs      Separated Pieces on U       Pieces Trapped in Slots
(Already paired on U)       (Different, Same, or White) (Corner or Edge stuck)
```

---

## 2. Archetype 1: Free / Connected Pairs

In this archetype, the corner and edge are already touching on the top layer (`U`).

### Case 1A: Connected Correctly (Ready for Insert)
The corner and edge form a matching 2x1 block with colors aligned.
- **Solution**: Move pair over slot, execute Right Insert `U (R U' R')` or Left Insert `U' (L' U L)`.
- **Move Count**: 3 to 4 moves.

### Case 1B: Connected Inverted (Mismatched Pair)
The corner and edge are touching on top, but the colors clash or the edge is backwards.
- **Solution**: Lift an open slot with `R`, break the pair with `U` or `U2`, restore the slot with `R'`, then solve as Archetype 2.

---

## 3. Archetype 2: Separated Pieces on the Top Layer

Both the corner and edge are on the top layer (`U`), but not touching. This is the most common archetype. Look at the **top sticker of the corner** and the **top sticker of the edge**:

### Case 2A: Different Top Colors (Corner side white, top colors contrast)
The corner has White on its side, and the top stickers of the corner and edge are **different colors** (e.g. Green on corner top, Red on edge top).

```
Goal: When you insert the corner with (R U' R'), the edge will automatically join it!
• Step 1: Position the edge so that lifting the slot joins them directly.
• Step 2: Execute (R U' R') to insert both pieces simultaneously.
```

### Case 2B: Same Top Colors (Corner side white, top colors match)
The corner has White on its side, and the top stickers of both pieces are the **same color** (e.g. Red on corner top, Red on edge top).

```
Goal: Pair the pieces side-by-side into a 2x1 block, then insert.
• Step 1: Hide the corner in an empty slot using (R U R') or (F' U' F).
• Step 2: Turn U to place the edge next to the hidden corner's landing spot.
• Step 3: Restore the corner with R' to form the 2x1 block on the top layer.
• Step 4: Insert the newly formed pair with (U R U' R').
```

### Case 2C: White Sticker Facing Up (`U`)
The corner has its White sticker pointing directly toward the ceiling (`U`).

When White faces up, match the edge to its side center first. Then use the corner to "sweep" over the edge:

## 4. Archetype 3: Pieces Trapped in Slots

If a corner or edge is stuck in the middle layer or bottom layer:

1. **Stuck in the target slot**: Use the 4-move trigger (Sexy Move: `R U R' U'`) to extract both pieces to the top layer (`U`).
2. **Stuck in a different slot**: Use `R U R'` or `L' U' L` to extract the trapped piece to `U` while immediately restoring that slot.
3. Once both pieces are on the top layer, identify whether they fall into **Archetype 1** or **Archetype 2** and solve!

## 8. Key Takeaways

- **41 cases reduce to 3 archetypes**: Free Pairs, Separated on `U`, and Trapped in Slots.
- **Look at the top colors**: Different top colors insert directly; same top colors pair side-by-side; white-up sweeps over the matched edge.
- **Extract trapped pieces first**: Use `R U R' U'` to bring stuck pieces to the top layer.
- **Understand the geometry**: Do not memorize isolated letters; understand *how* hiding and pairing works.
