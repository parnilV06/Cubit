With the first two layers (F2L) completely solved, you now turn your attention to the **final layer (Yellow face)**.

The first goal on the last layer is creating a **Yellow Cross** (a yellow plus sign) on the top face.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> During this step, **ignore all yellow corner pieces**. Focus exclusively on the 4 yellow edge pieces. Even if the corners are yellow, treat the top face strictly as a Dot, L-Shape, Line, or Cross based only on its edges.

---

## 1. The 4 Yellow Edge States

Look down at the top yellow face. You will see one of four possible edge patterns:

```
  1. DOT               2. L-SHAPE               3. LINE              4. CROSS
  . . .                 . Y .                    . . .                . Y .
  . Y .                 Y Y .                    Y Y Y                Y Y Y
  . . .                 . . .                    . . .                . Y .
(0 Edges)             (2 Adjacent)             (2 Opposite)         (4 Edges - Solved!)
```

The algorithm below advances you through this exact pipeline:
$$\text{Dot} \longrightarrow \text{L-Shape} \longrightarrow \text{Line} \longrightarrow \text{Yellow Cross}$$

---

## 2. The Yellow Cross Algorithm

The entire progression uses a single intuitive 6-move sequence:

```notation
F R U R' U' F'
```

Notice that this is simply:
1. Turn the Front face clockwise: **`F`**
2. Execute the Right Trigger: **`R U R' U'`**
3. Restore the Front face counter-clockwise: **`F'`**

## 3. How to Hold the Cube for Each State

Before performing `F R U R' U' F'`, you must hold the cube in the correct orientation:

| Current Pattern | How to Hold the Cube | Next Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dot** | Hold the cube facing any side with Yellow on Top | Advances to **L-Shape** |
| **L-Shape (Hook)** | Hold so the two yellow edges point **Top and Left** (12 o'clock and 9 o'clock) | Advances to **Line** |
| **Line** | Hold so the yellow line runs **Horizontally** (left-to-right) | Advances to **Yellow Cross** |
| **Cross** | All 4 yellow edges face upward | **Step Complete!** Move to Lesson 6 |

> [!WARNING]
> If you hold the **Line vertically** instead of horizontally, the algorithm will break the line and return you to an L-shape! Always ensure the line runs from left to right.
