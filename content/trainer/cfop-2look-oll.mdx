In the third stage of CFOP, your goal is **OLL (Orientation of the Last Layer)**: make the entire top face of the cube solid yellow.

Full OLL contains **57 unique cases** and 57 algorithms. Trying to memorize all 57 at once can overwhelm intermediate cubers.

Instead, the speedcubing community uses **2-Look OLL**. By splitting the orientation into two sequential stages, you can solve every single last-layer orientation using **only 9 total algorithms**!

```
[ Solved F2L ]
      ↓
[ Stage 1: Edge Orientation (EO) ]
Orient top-layer edges into a Yellow Cross (3 algorithms)
      ↓
[ Stage 2: Corner Orientation (CO) ]
Orient top-layer corners to complete the solid Yellow face (7 algorithms)
      ↓
[ Completed Yellow Top Face ]
```

> [!NOTE]
> During OLL, only focus on making the **top face yellow**. Do not worry about whether the side colors of the top layer match; that is handled in the final stage (PLL).

---

## 1. Stage 1: Edge Orientation (3 Cases)

Look at the yellow stickers on the four top-layer edges:

### 1. Bar Case (Horizontal Line)
Hold the bar horizontally across the top face:
- **Algorithm**: `F R U R' U' F'` (Standard `F` + Sexy Move + `F'`)

### 2. L-Shape Case (Angle in Back-Left)
Hold the two oriented yellow edges in the back and left positions (top-left angle):
- **Algorithm**: `f R U R' U' f'` (Wide `f` turn + Sexy Move + Wide `f'`)

### 3. Dot Case (No Yellow Edges Oriented)
Execute the Bar algorithm, adjust `U`, and execute the L-Shape algorithm:
- **Algorithm**: `F R U R' U' F' f R U R' U' f'`

## 2. Stage 2: Corner Orientation (The 7 Cross Cases)

Once the Yellow Cross is formed, count how many yellow corner stickers are facing up on the top face. There are exactly 7 possible cases:

### Case 1: Sune (1 Corner Oriented)
- **Recognition**: Exactly 1 yellow corner on top. Place that corner in the **Front-Left** position. The Front-Right corner has its yellow sticker facing the **Front**.
- **Algorithm**: `R U R' U R U2 R'`

### Case 2: Anti-Sune (1 Corner Oriented)
- **Recognition**: Exactly 1 yellow corner on top. Place that corner in the **Back-Right** position. The Front-Right corner has its yellow sticker facing the **Right**.
- **Algorithm**: `R U2 R' U' R U' R'`

### Case 3: H Case / Double Headlights (0 Corners Oriented)
- **Recognition**: 0 yellow corners on top. 2 yellow headlights on the front, 2 yellow headlights on the back.
- **Algorithm**: `R U2 R' U' R U R' U' R U' R'`

### Case 4: Pi Case (0 Corners Oriented)
- **Recognition**: 0 yellow corners on top. 2 yellow headlights on the left side; the other two yellow corners point in opposite directions on the right.
- **Algorithm**: `R U2 R2 U' R2 U' R2 U2 R`

### Case 5: Headlights / U Case (2 Corners Oriented)
- **Recognition**: 2 yellow corners on top (forming a 2x1 yellow bar). The remaining two corners have yellow headlights facing the **Front**.
- **Algorithm**: `R2 D R' U2 R D' R' U2 R'`

### Case 6: Chameleon / T Case (2 Corners Oriented)
- **Recognition**: 2 yellow corners on top. The remaining two yellow stickers face outward in opposite directions on the sides.
- **Algorithm**: `r U R' U' r' F R F'`

### Case 7: Bowtie / L Case (2 Corners Oriented Diagonally)
- **Recognition**: 2 yellow corners on top placed diagonally across from each other.
- **Algorithm**: `F' r U R' U' r' F R`

## 5. Key Takeaways

- **OLL makes the entire top face yellow**: Orientation only; do not worry about side colors yet.
- **2-Look splits OLL into 2 stages**: Stage 1 = Orient Edges (Cross), Stage 2 = Orient Corners.
- **Reduces 57 algorithms down to 9**: Allows sub-25 second CFOP solves with minimal memorization.
- **Master Sune and Anti-Sune**: Foundation for both OLL and future puzzle methods like 2x2 Ortega.
