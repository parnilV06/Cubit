You now have a fully aligned Yellow Cross matching all side centers.

The next step is to place the **four yellow corner pieces into their correct spatial positions**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Position vs. Orientation**: A corner is **correctly positioned** if its 3 colors match the 3 surrounding center pieces, **even if the yellow sticker is facing sideways**. Do not worry if the corner is twisted; orientation is solved in the next step!

---

## 1. What a Correctly Positioned Corner Looks Like

Look at each of the 4 top corners. A corner between the **Yellow, Green, and Red** centers is correctly positioned if it contains **Yellow, Green, and Red stickers**—regardless of which direction the yellow sticker points:

```
[ Correct Position (Untwisted) ]  →  Yellow on Top, Green on Front, Red on Right
[ Correct Position (Twisted) ]    →  Red on Top, Yellow on Front, Green on Right  (STILL VALID!)
[ Wrong Position ]                →  Contains Blue/Orange stickers (Belongs elsewhere)
```

---

## 2. Inspecting the 4 Corners

Check how many corners are currently in their correct positions:

1. **All 4 corners are in position**: You are ready for the final step! Skip directly to Lesson 8.
2. **Exactly 1 corner is in position**: Hold the cube so the correct corner is in the **Front-Right-Up (`UFR`) position**.
3. **0 corners are in position**: Hold the cube in any orientation and apply the algorithm below once. This will immediately place 1 corner in its correct position.

---

## 3. The Corner Cycling Algorithm (Niklas)

To cycle the remaining three corners while keeping the front-right corner locked in place, use the **Niklas algorithm**:

```notation
U R U' L' U R' U' L
```

Watch how this sequence cycles the three unsolved corners in a triangle:

> [!TIP]
> **The Easy Rhythm**: Notice the alternating pattern:
> 1. Turn `U`, lift the right side (`R`)
> 2. Turn `U'`, lift the left side (`L'`)
> 3. Turn `U`, restore the right side (`R'`)
> 4. Turn `U'`, restore the left side (`L`)

---

## 4. How to Execute the Step

1. Identify the **one corner** that is in the correct slot.
2. Hold the cube so this corner is in the **front-right (`UFR`)** position.
3. Execute **`U R U' L' U R' U' L`**.
4. Check the other 3 corners:
   - If all 4 are now in their correct positions, proceed to Lesson 8.
   - If they are still not in place, execute **`U R U' L' U R' U' L`** a second time from the exact same position. All 4 corners are now guaranteed to be in their correct slots!

- [x] The aligned Yellow Cross and bottom two layers (F2L) remain intact.
- [x] All 4 top corners sit between their 3 matching centers (even if some corners are twisted).
