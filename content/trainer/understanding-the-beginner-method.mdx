Most people who try to solve a Rubik's Cube make the same fundamental mistake: they try to solve **one face at a time**.

They spend minutes completing the white face, only to realize that every side sticker is scrambled—and solving the next face immediately destroys their progress.

A Rubik's Cube is not made of individual colored stickers. It is made of **26 solid pieces** (centers, edges, and corners) with fixed color relationships. To solve the cube, you must solve entire **layers**.

> [!NOTE]
> The **Layer-by-Layer (LBL)** method is the universal foundation of all cubing. It breaks the puzzle into 7 manageable milestones where each step builds upon the previous without destroying it.

---

## 1. The 7-Step Solving Roadmap

Here is the exact progression you will follow in this module:

| Step | Milestone | Goal | What Gets Solved |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **White Cross** | Form a white cross where each white edge matches its adjacent side center | 4 Bottom Edges |
| **2** | **First-Layer Corners** | Insert 4 white corners to complete the entire white face and bottom layer | Layer 1 Complete |
| **3** | **Middle Layer** | Place 4 non-yellow edges into the middle layer slots | Layers 1 & 2 Complete (F2L) |
| **4** | **Yellow Cross** | Orient top yellow edges to form a yellow cross | Top Edge Orientation |
| **5** | **Yellow Edges** | Permute yellow cross edges until side colors match their centers | Top Edge Permutation |
| **6** | **Position Yellow Corners** | Move top 4 corners into their correct spatial positions | Top Corner Permutation |
| **7** | **Orient Yellow Corners** | Rotate final corners so yellow faces upward to complete the solve | 100% Solved Cube |

## 3. The Golden Rules of Beginner Solving

Before learning individual steps, commit these three principles to memory:

1. **Centers Never Move**: The center piece on each face defines that face's color. White will always be opposite Yellow, Green opposite Blue, and Red opposite Orange.
2. **Algorithms Are Surgical Tools**: When you execute an algorithm like `R U R' U'`, pieces will temporarily move out of place during the turn sequence. Complete the full sequence, and previously solved layers will restore themselves automatically.
3. **Keep a Consistent Reference Orientation**: Unless a lesson specifically tells you to rotate the cube with `x`, `y`, or `z`, keep the same face facing you throughout each step.

---

## 4. Summary & Key Takeaways

- Solve by **layers**, not by faces.
- The 7-step method builds sequentially: **White Cross $\to$ First Layer $\to$ Middle Layer $\to$ Yellow Cross $\to$ Yellow Edges $\to$ Position Corners $\to$ Orient Corners**.
- Each step preserves all progress made in earlier steps.
