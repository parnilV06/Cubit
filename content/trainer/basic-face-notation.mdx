To solve a Rubik's Cube, learn algorithms, and talk to other speedcubers, you need to know **cube notation**.

Cube notation is the universal language of the cube. Instead of saying *"turn the right side upwards and turn the top to the left"*, we write concise letters like **`R U`**.

Every move you will ever make is built from these basic building blocks.

> [!NOTE]
> Standard speedcubing notation is defined by the **World Cube Association (WCA)** and is recognized by cubers and competition timers worldwide.

---

## 1. The Six Face Letters

A standard 3x3 Rubik's Cube has six faces. Each face is identified by a single capital letter corresponding to its position relative to your viewpoint:

| Letter | Face | Position | Standard Color (with Green Front) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **U** | **Up** | The top face | White |
| **D** | **Down** | The bottom face | Yellow |
| **F** | **Front** | The face directly facing you | Green |
| **B** | **Back** | The face opposite the front | Blue |
| **R** | **Right** | The right-hand face | Red |
| **L** | **Left** | The left-hand face | Orange |

## 2. The Clockwise Rule

The most important rule in cube notation is:

> **A single letter means turning that face 90 degrees clockwise, as if you were looking directly at that face.**

Because you are viewing the cube from the front, turning some faces can feel counter-intuitive at first:

- **`R` (Right)**: Turning the right face clockwise moves the right-hand layer **upwards** (away from you).
- **`L` (Left)**: Turning the left face clockwise moves the left-hand layer **downwards** (towards you).
- **`U` (Up)**: Turning the top face clockwise rotates the top layer **to the left**.
- **`D` (Down)**: Turning the bottom face clockwise rotates the bottom layer **to the right**.
- **`F` (Front)**: Turning the front face clockwise turns the front layer like the hands of a clock.
- **`B` (Back)**: Turning the back face clockwise rotates the back layer from its perspective.

> [!TIP]
> If you are ever unsure which way a turn goes, imagine holding the cube so you are looking directly at that face, then turn it clockwise like a wall clock.

## 4. Reading Move Sequences (Algorithms)

An **algorithm** is simply a sequence of moves written from left to right. You execute each move in order.

For example, the most famous sequence in speedcubing is the **Right Trigger** (often called the *Sexy Move*):

```notation
R U R' U'
```

*Note: In the next lesson, you will learn that the apostrophe `'` denotes a counter-clockwise "prime" turn.*

## 5. Summary & Key Takeaways

- **6 Faces**: `U` (Up), `D` (Down), `F` (Front), `B` (Back), `R` (Right), `L` (Left).
- **Single Letter = Clockwise 90°**: Always determined by looking straight at that specific face.
- **Opposing pairs turn in opposite visual directions**: `R` moves up while `L` moves down from the front perspective.
- **Algorithms**: Read and executed sequentially from left to right.
