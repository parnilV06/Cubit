Modern speedcubes have evolved far beyond the simple single-screw designs of the past. Today's flagship puzzles feature dual-adjustment systems, magnetic levitation (**MagLev**), ball-cores with omnidirectional positioning, and multi-step magnet adjustment dials.

Understanding how these systems interact empowers you to tune your cube to match your exact grip, turning speed, and fingertrick style.

---

## 1. The Anatomy of Modern Adjustment Systems

Modern cubes separate mechanical tuning into distinct, independent variables:

```
[ Screw Depth (Tension) ]      →  Controls how far pieces can pull away from the core (Corner Cutting vs. Pops).
[ Spring Compression / MagLev ] →  Controls turning resistance, springiness, and flexibility.
[ Piece Magnet Strength ]      →  Controls the tactile "snap" when a layer completes a 90° turn.
[ Ball-Core Alignment ]        →  Magnets between the core and corner feet pull layers into alignment from up to 30°.
```

---

## 2. Tension vs. Compression vs. MagLev Explained

Many cubers confuse **tension** (screw depth) with **compression** (elasticity). Here is the difference:

### 1. Center Screw Depth (Tension / Distance)
- **What it does**: Determines the maximum travel distance of the center piece outward from the core.
- **Looser (Screw turned counter-clockwise)**: Increases corner cutting angle, but makes the cube more prone to corner-twists and pops.
- **Tighter (Screw turned clockwise)**: Keeps pieces compact and prevents pops, but restricts corner cutting and can cause catching.

### 2. Spring Compression (Elasticity)
- **What it does**: Compresses the spring independent of the screw depth via stepped notched dials (like MoYu dual-adjustment or Tornado V3 ring dials).
- **Lower compression**: Soft, flexible, and light turning feel.
- **Higher compression**: Stiff, snappy, and highly stable feel that snaps quickly back to square.

### 3. MagLev (Magnetic Levitation)
- **What it does**: Replaces traditional metal coil springs with two opposing ring magnets placed facing like-poles (`N-N` or `S-S`).
- **Benefits**:
  - **Zero Spring Noise**: Completely eliminates metal friction and spring squeaking.
  - **Reduced Friction**: Provides a frictionless, lightning-fast turning experience.
  - **Permanent Elasticity**: Magnets never lose tension or degrade over time like metal wire springs.

## 3. The 4-Step Tuning Workflow

When tuning your cube, follow this systematic approach:

```
[ Step 1: Diagnose the Symptom ]
Identify what feels wrong during solves (e.g., "The cube is catching on turns," or "It feels too loose and wobbles").

[ Step 2: Adjust ONE Setting Across All 6 Faces ]
Never change multiple settings at once. Change the setting by 1 click on all 6 faces (White, Yellow, Green, Blue, Red, Orange) so the cube remains perfectly symmetrical.

[ Step 3: Test with 10 Solves ]
Complete 10 full solves or timed scrambles to let your hands adapt to the new setting.

[ Step 4: Re-evaluate ]
If the problem is solved, stop. If you need further refinement, adjust the next setting.
```

## 4. Troubleshooting Common Cube Behavior

| What You Feel | Root Cause | Recommended Adjustment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cube locks up and catches frequently** | Center distance is too tight or elasticity is too stiff | Loosen the center screw by 0.5 turns or decrease compression by 1 click |
| **Cube feels flimsy, wobbles, or pops pieces** | Center distance is too loose | Tighten center screw by 0.5 turns across all faces |
| **Cube is uncontrollable and overshoots turns** | Magnet strength too weak or spring compression too loose | Increase magnet strength by 1 setting or increase spring compression |
| **Turns feel heavy, sluggish, and fatiguing** | Compression is too high or lube is too heavy | Lower spring compression by 1 to 2 clicks or clean internal tracks |

## 5. Disassembly & Magnetic Core Safety

If you need to open your magnetic cube for cleaning or deep adjustment:

1. **Be Gentle with Corner Stems**: Flagship cubes feature magnetic stems at the bottom of corner pieces that reach into the central ball-core. Never yank a piece forcefully. Rotate the top layer 45° and lift upward smoothly.
2. **Beware of Magnet Polarity**: If you open individual edge or corner piece caps, ensure you do not flip internal magnets upside down. Flipped magnets will repel neighboring pieces instead of attracting them!
3. **Keep Screws Balanced**: When adjusting screws manually, use a small millimeter ruler or counting half-turns to ensure all 6 centers have identical travel distances.

---

## 6. Summary Checklist

- **Tension (Screw)** controls piece travel and corner cutting.
- **Compression (Notch / Dial)** controls spring stiffness and stability.
- **MagLev** replaces springs with repelling magnets for friction-free speed.
- **Ball-Cores** add corner-to-core magnetic attraction for auto-alignment.
- Always make symmetrical adjustments across all 6 faces.
