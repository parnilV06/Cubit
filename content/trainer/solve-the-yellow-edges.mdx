Now that you have a Yellow Cross on the top face, you must make sure that its **side colors match their respective centers**.

Just like the White Cross in Step 2, a complete yellow cross requires that the Yellow-Green edge matches Green, Yellow-Red matches Red, Yellow-Blue matches Blue, and Yellow-Orange matches Orange.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The Yellow Cross edges may currently be scrambled around the top layer. In this lesson, you will swap the mismatched edges into their correct positions while preserving the yellow face and bottom two layers.

---

## 1. Inspecting Top Edge Alignment

1. Hold the cube with **Yellow on Top (`U`)** and **White on Bottom (`D`)**.
2. **Turn the `U` layer** and check how many side colors of the yellow cross match their centers.
3. You will always be able to align either **2 matched edges** or **all 4 matched edges**:

- **All 4 Matched**: All edges are already in their correct places! You can skip directly to Lesson 7.
- **2 Adjacent Matched**: Two matching edges are next to each other (e.g., Green and Red).
- **2 Opposite Matched**: Two matching edges are across from each other (e.g., Green and Blue).

---

## 2. The Edge Swap Algorithm (The Sune)

To swap the mismatched edges into place without breaking your solved layers, use the classic **Sune algorithm**:

```notation
R U R' U R U2 R' U
```

Watch how this sequence swaps the **Front** and **Left** edges while keeping the **Back** and **Right** edges locked in place:

## 3. How to Hold the Cube

Before applying `R U R' U R U2 R' U`, position your cube according to your case:

### Case 1: Two Adjacent Matched Edges
1. Turn the `U` layer until 2 adjacent edges match their side centers.
2. Hold the cube so one matched edge is at the **BACK** and the other is on the **RIGHT**.
3. Execute: **`R U R' U R U2 R' U`**.
4. All 4 yellow edges will now match their respective side centers!

```
               [ BACK: Matched Edge ]
                         ↑
[ LEFT: Swap Target ] ← [ U ] → [ RIGHT: Matched Edge ]
                         ↓
              [ FRONT: Swap Target ]
```

### Case 2: Two Opposite Matched Edges
1. Hold the cube so one matched edge is in the **FRONT** and the other is in the **BACK**.
2. Execute: **`R U R' U R U2 R' U`**.
3. Now turn the `U` layer—the cube has transformed into **Case 1 (Adjacent Matched)**!
4. Position the two matched edges at Back and Right, and execute the algorithm once more.
