The **Snake** pattern (often called the **Anaconda**) is one of the most mesmerizing aesthetic transformations possible on a 3x3 cube.

Instead of creating isolated blocks or symmetric checkerboards, the algorithm weaves a continuous, unbroken single-tile path of contrasting colors that winds gracefully across all six faces of the cube.

> [!NOTE]
> Ensure your cube is **fully solved** before applying the algorithm. Hold the cube with White on Top and Green on Front for matching color orientation.

---

## 1. The 14-Move Snake Algorithm

Execute the following 14-move sequence from a solved cube:

```notation
L U B' U' R L' B R' F B' D R D' F'
```

## 3. How to Restore the Cube

Because the Snake algorithm contains a mixture of clockwise and counter-clockwise quarter turns, it is not self-inverse. 

To restore your cube back to a fully solved state, execute the exact **inverse sequence** (the moves performed in reverse order with opposite turn directions):

```notation
F D R' D' B F' R B' L R' U B U' L'
```

## 5. Key Takeaways

- **Continuous Ribbon**: Wraps a single-tile contrasting path around the entire cube.
- **Algorithm (14 moves)**: `L U B' U' R L' B R' F B' D R D' F'`.
- **Restoration**: Execute the inverse sequence `F D R' D' B F' R B' L R' U B U' L'`.
- **Great for Notation**: Strengthens your ability to read and execute mixed face turns smoothly.
