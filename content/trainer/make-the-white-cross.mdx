The first physical step in solving a Rubik's Cube is building the **White Cross**.

However, you must not just create any white plus sign. You must create an **aligned White Cross**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A valid White Cross requires that all four white edge pieces match **both** the white center and their adjacent side centers (Green, Red, Blue, Orange).

---

## 1. Correct Cross vs. Incorrect Cross

Take a close look at the difference between a solved cross and an incorrectly assembled cross:

| Type | White Face | Side Center Alignment | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Correct Cross** | 4 White Edges on White Center | Every edge side-color matches its center (e.g. White-Green matches Green) | **Ready for Step 2** |
| **Fake Cross** | 4 White Edges on White Center | Side colors do not match centers (e.g. White-Green is on Red center) | **Broken (Must Fix)** |

## 2. The Daisy Strategy (Beginner-Friendly Method)

The easiest and most reliable way to construct a perfect White Cross is using the **Daisy Method**.

Instead of trying to solve the cross directly on the bottom face, you first gather the four white edges around the **Yellow center** on top (creating a yellow-and-white "daisy" flower), and then send them down one by one.

```
Scrambled Cube → Build Daisy on Yellow Center → Match Side Centers → 180° Turn (F2) → Aligned White Cross
```

### Step A: Form the Daisy
1. Hold the cube so the **Yellow center is on Top (`U`)** and the **White center is on Bottom (`D`)**.
2. Locate the 4 edge pieces with white stickers.
3. Bring each white edge up to the top face around the yellow center. No algorithms are needed:
   - If a white edge is in the middle layer, a single turn (`R`, `L`, or `F`) brings it to the top.
   - If a white edge is on the bottom face, turn that face 180 degrees (`F2` or `R2`) to bring it up.
   - If an edge is flipped (white sticker facing the side rather than the top), turn `F'`, rotate the top layer with `U'`, and turn `R` to insert it.

---

## 3. Transferring the Daisy to the White Cross

Once all four white petals surround the yellow center, transferring them to the bottom face is straightforward:

1. **Pick one white edge** on the top layer (for example, the **White-Green** edge).
2. Look at its **side color** (Green).
3. **Turn the `U` layer** until that side color aligns directly over its matching side center (Green over Green).
4. **Turn that face 180 degrees (`F2`)**. The White-Green edge moves to the bottom face, perfectly aligned!
5. **Repeat** this exact process for the remaining three edges (Red, Blue, Orange).

## 4. Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Ignoring Side Colors**: If you turn `F2` before matching the edge's side color with its center, your cross will be scrambled. Always match the side center first!
- **Worrying About Corner Pieces**: During this step, ignore all corner pieces. Even if white corners end up on the top or bottom face, only focus on the 4 cross edges.
- **Accidentally Flipping the Cube**: Keep the Yellow center on top and White on the bottom throughout the Daisy process.

Before moving to the next lesson, verify:
- [x] There is a white cross on the bottom (`D`) face.
- [x] The White-Green edge matches the Green center.
- [x] The White-Red edge matches the Red center.
- [x] The White-Blue edge matches the Blue center.
- [x] The White-Orange edge matches the Orange center.
