The **Checkerboard** is the most celebrated and instantly recognizable pattern in the history of twisty puzzles.

On every single face, the four corner stickers and the center sticker display one color, while the four edge stickers display the exact opposite color (White alternates with Yellow, Green with Blue, and Red with Orange).

> [!NOTE]
> All patterns in this module start from a **fully solved cube**. If your cube is currently scrambled, solve it first or use the interactive 3D players below.

---

## 1. The Classic Algorithm: 6 Double Turns

The standard Checkerboard algorithm applies 180° double turns across all three spatial dimensions:

```
[ X-Axis (Left & Right) ]  →  R2  L2
[ Y-Axis (Up & Down) ]     →  U2  D2
[ Z-Axis (Front & Back) ]  →  F2  B2
```

## 2. The 3-Move Slice Shortcut: `M2 E2 S2`

If you are comfortable with slice turns (moving only the middle layer between outer faces), you can create the identical checkerboard in just **three moves**:

```
[ Move 1: M2 ] → Turn the vertical Middle slice 180° (between L and R)
[ Move 2: E2 ] → Turn the horizontal Equator slice 180° (between U and D)
[ Move 3: S2 ] → Turn the Standing front slice 180° (between F and B)
```

## 4. How to Restore the Cube

One of the most elegant properties of the Checkerboard algorithm is that it is **self-inverse**.

Because rotating any face by 180° twice brings it a full 360° back to its original orientation:
$$\text{Move} \times 2 = 360^\circ = \text{Solved}$$

To return your cube back to solved, simply execute the exact same algorithm a second time:
```notation
R2 L2 U2 D2 F2 B2
```
or with slice moves:
```notation
M2 E2 S2
```

## 6. Key Takeaways

- **Opposite Color Contrast**: Swaps edges with their opposite face colors across all 6 sides.
- **Classic Algorithm**: `R2 L2 U2 D2 F2 B2` (6 moves).
- **Slice Shortcut**: `M2 E2 S2` (3 moves).
- **Self-Inverse**: Executing the sequence a second time immediately restores the solved cube.
