You have explored every individual engine component of the CFOP speedsolving method:
- **Cross**: Planned during inspection, solved on the bottom face (`D`).
- **F2L**: Four corner-edge pairs joined and inserted with zero rotations.
- **OLL**: All eight top-layer yellow stickers oriented to face up.
- **PLL**: Corners and edges permuted into their solved slots, completed with AUF.

Now, it is time to bring all four stages together into a single, seamless, high-speed performance.

> [!NOTE]
> A great CFOP speedsolve is not a frantic sprint of four separate steps. It is a continuous, unbroken river of motion where each stage flows directly into the next without stopping.

## 1. The Complete Solve Architecture

```
[ Phase 1: 15-Second Inspection ]
1. Locate all 4 cross edges.
2. Calculate the 6-move cross solution.
3. Locate the first F2L corner and edge on top.
                 ↓ (Hands on Timer)
[ Phase 2: Cross Execution (2.0s) ]
Execute cross on bottom face without looking.
Eyes lock onto Pair 1.
                 ↓ (Zero-Pause Transition)
[ Phase 3: F2L (10.0s) ]
• Pair 1: Back-Right Slot (Rotationless)
• Pair 2: Back-Left Slot (Rotationless)
• Pair 3: Front-Left Slot
• Pair 4: Front-Right Slot
During Pair 4 insert, eyes predict OLL shape!
                 ↓ (Zero-Pause Transition)
[ Phase 4: OLL (3.0s) ]
Execute OLL algorithm.
Eyes scan perimeter for PLL headlights and bars!
                 ↓ (Zero-Pause Transition)
[ Phase 5: PLL + AUF (3.5s) ]
Execute PLL algorithm.
Perform final AUF flick (U / U' / U2).
                 ↓ (Hands slap timer)
[ Solved Cube: ~18.5 Seconds | ~52 Total Moves ]
```

## 3. Time Distribution of a Sub-20 Solve

Where should your time be spent during a typical 20-second CFOP solve?

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Cross: 2.5s (12%)  │ F2L: 10.0s (50%) │ OLL: 3.5s │ PLL: 4.0s │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **F2L takes 50% of the entire solve**: If you want to drop from 30 seconds to 20 seconds, focus 80% of your practice time on F2L efficiency, back-slot inserts, and lookahead.
- **Cross should take under 2.5 seconds**: Achieved entirely by planning the solution before starting the timer.
- **Last layer should take under 7.5 seconds**: Achieved through fast recognition of 2-Look or Full OLL/PLL algorithms.

## 4. The 3 Golden Rules of CFOP Mastery

### Rule 1: Never Turn Faster Than You Can Look Ahead
If you turn at 8 TPS but freeze for 2 seconds after every pair to find the next piece, your effective speed is terrible. Slow your turning down to 3.5 TPS until you can solve all four pairs with zero pauses.

### Rule 2: Solve Back Slots First
Always prioritize the Back-Right and Back-Left slots. Keeping the front slots open preserves 100% visibility for the rest of your solve.

### Rule 3: Practice in Targeted Blocks
Do not just do mindless timed solves. Divide your training sessions:
- **15 minutes**: Cross planning (blindfolded cross execution).
- **20 minutes**: Slow-turning F2L lookahead (metronome practice).
- **15 minutes**: PLL and OLL algorithm drilling.
- **10 minutes**: Timed full solves.
