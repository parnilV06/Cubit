The **World Cube Association (WCA)** is the global governing organization for official speedcubing competitions. Founded in 2004, the WCA oversees tournaments in over 140 countries, maintaining official world records, regional rankings, and standardized competitive regulations.

Whether you aspire to compete for national titles or simply want to attend a local meetup to benchmark your personal progress, understanding the WCA ecosystem connects you to the worldwide cubing community.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Independent Platform Disclaimer**: Cubit is an independent educational platform. Cubit is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by the World Cube Association. Solves, ratings, and leaderboards on Cubit are platform-specific and do not represent official WCA results. For official rules, rankings, and event schedules, visit the [official WCA website](https://www.worldcubeassociation.org).

## 1. The 17 Official WCA Events

As of 2026, the World Cube Association recognizes **17 official events** across various puzzle families:

```
[ Classic NxN Cubes ]
• 3x3x3 Cube (Flagship Event)
• 2x2x2 Cube
• 4x4x4 Cube
• 5x5x5 Cube
• 6x6x6 Cube
• 7x7x7 Cube

[ Specialized Formats ]
• 3x3x3 One-Handed (3OH)
• 3x3x3 Fewest Moves (FMC - 60 minutes to write the shortest solution)

[ Non-Cubic Puzzles ]
• Pyraminx (Tetrahedron)
• Megaminx (12-Sided Dodecahedron)
• Skewb (Corner-turning cube)
• Square-1 (Shape-shifting puzzle)
• Rubik's Clock (Mechanical dial puzzle)

[ Blindfolded Events ]
• 3x3x3 Blindfolded (3BLD)
• 4x4x4 Blindfolded (4BLD)
• 5x5x5 Blindfolded (5BLD)
• 3x3x3 Multi-Blind (MBLD - Memorizing and solving multiple cubes in 60 minutes)
```

> [!NOTE]
> In late 2025, the WCA Board announced upcoming event transitions: **Face Turning Octahedron (FTO)** will become an official event starting January 2027, while **Clock** will conclude its official tenure following the 2027 WCA World Championship.

## 2. Key Roles in an Official Competition

Every sanctioned WCA competition runs on a volunteer-powered framework with clear responsibilities:

| Role | Responsibilities |
| :--- | :--- |
| **WCA Delegate** | The official WCA official on site. Ensures all WCA regulations are strictly upheld, settles rule disputes, and submits verified results to the official database. |
| **Organizer** | Manages venue logistics, event schedules, registration limits, and competition equipment. |
| **Scrambler** | Applies official computer-generated scramble sequences (generated by WCA's certified software, TNoodle) in a secure holding area. |
| **Judge** | Escorts the puzzle to the competitor, monitors the 15-second inspection timer, observes the solve, and records the final result on the score sheet. |
| **Runner** | Transports scrambled puzzles in opaque covers from the scrambling table to solving stations. |

## 3. How Scoring & Formats Work

Official competitions use standardized round formats to evaluate consistency:

### Average of 5 (Ao5)
- The most common format for fast events (3x3, 2x2, 4x4, Pyraminx, Skewb).
- You complete **5 separate solves**.
- Your single fastest time and single slowest time are **dropped** (trimmed).
- The remaining 3 middle times are averaged together to compute your official **Average of 5**.

### Mean of 3 (Mo3)
- Used for longer puzzles (6x6, 7x7, 3BLD, FMC).
- You complete **3 solves**, and the simple arithmetic average of all 3 times is calculated.

## 4. Understanding WCA Penalties

To ensure strict fairness, official regulations define exact penalty thresholds:

```
[ +2 Second Penalty ]
• Starting the timer between 15.01 and 17.00 seconds of inspection.
• Stopping the timer with a face misaligned by more than 45 degrees.
• Touching the cube with your hands while stopping the timer.

[ DNF (Did Not Finish) ]
• Starting the solve after 17.00 seconds of inspection.
• Stopping the timer before the puzzle is fully solved (more than one move away).
• Looking under the blindfold during blindfolded events.
```

## 5. Official WCA ID & Profile

When you participate in your first official WCA competition:
1. You are assigned a permanent, unique **WCA ID** (for example, `2024SHAR01`).
2. A public athlete profile is created on `worldcubeassociation.org`.
3. All your official single times, averages, regional rankings, national rankings, and podium finishes are tracked for life.
