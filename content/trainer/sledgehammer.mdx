Alongside the Sexy Move, the second essential 4-move trigger in speedcubing is the **Sledgehammer**: **`R' F R F'`**.

While the Sexy Move operates entirely on the Right and Upper layers (`R` and `U`), the Sledgehammer turns the Right and Front layers (`R` and `F`). This front-face interaction makes it exceptionally powerful for flipping top-layer edges while inserting corner-edge pairs.

> [!NOTE]
> Speedcubers affectionately named the sequence "Sledgehammer" because the opening move (`R'`) pulls the right side down like swinging a hammer, followed by the punchy front-face snap (`F`).

---

## 1. Step-by-Step Movement Breakdown

The Sledgehammer consists of four fast moves:

```
[ Move 1: R' ] → Pull Right face counter-clockwise (drops slot down)
[ Move 2: F ]  → Push Front face clockwise (snaps front layer right)
[ Move 3: R ]  → Push Right face clockwise (lifts slot back up)
[ Move 4: F' ] → Push Front face counter-clockwise (restores Front face)
```

## 2. Sexy Move vs. Sledgehammer

Understanding the difference between these two triggers unlocks advanced speedcubing control:

| Feature | Sexy Move (`R U R' U'`) | Sledgehammer (`R' F R F'`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Active Layers** | Right (`R`) and Up (`U`) | Right (`R`) and Front (`F`) |
| **Top Edge Orientation** | Preserves edge flip state | **Flips 2 top-layer edges** |
| **Primary Use** | Fast cycling & basic inserts | **Edge orientation control (EO)** |
| **Cyclic Order** | 6 repetitions return to solved | 6 repetitions return to solved |

---

## 3. The Inverse Trigger: Hedgeslammer (`F R' F' R`)

Every algorithm has an exact mathematical inverse that undoes its transformations. The inverse of Sledgehammer is called the **Hedgeslammer**:

```
Sledgehammer:  R'  F   R   F'
     Inverse:  F   R'  F'  R   (Hedgeslammer)
```

If you execute one Sledgehammer on a solved cube, executing one Hedgeslammer will immediately return the cube to 100% solved:

## 4. Fingertrick Execution

To execute the Sledgehammer quickly without whole-hand regrips:
1. **`R'`**: Pull the right face down using your right wrist and thumb.
2. **`F`**: Push the top-right corner of the front face down and left using your right index finger.
3. **`R`**: Push the right face up with your right wrist.
4. **`F'`**: Push the front face up and right using your right thumb.

## 5. Where Does Sledgehammer Appear in Solving?

- **F2L Edge Orientation**: Inserting an F2L pair with Sledgehammer (`R' F R F'`) instead of standard `U R U' R'` forces a Yellow Cross on top (saving a step in OLL!).
- **OLL Case 33**: Solved directly as Sexy Move + Sledgehammer: `(R U R' U') (R' F R F')`.
- **T-Permutation**: The second half of T-Perm incorporates a modified Sledgehammer sequence to permute corners.

---

## 6. Key Takeaways

- **Notation**: `R' F R F'`.
- **Flips top edges**: Changes top-layer edge orientation during pair insertion.
- **Hedgeslammer is the inverse**: `F R' F' R` immediately restores one Sledgehammer.
- **Cyclic Order = 6**: 6 repetitions from solved returns to a 100% solved cube.
