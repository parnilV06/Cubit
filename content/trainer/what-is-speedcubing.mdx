Solving a Rubik's Cube for the first time is a rewarding puzzle milestone. **Speedcubing** takes that milestone and turns it into a fast-paced sport of pattern recognition, finger dexterity, and mental efficiency.

Speedcubers do not solve cubes through trial and error. They use structured systems, fluid finger movements, and instant visual recognition to solve puzzles in seconds.

> [!NOTE]
> In official World Cube Association (WCA) competitions, speedcubers solve identical computer-generated scrambles using standardized electronic timing pads.

---

## 1. Solving vs. Speedsolving

There is a big difference between casually solving a cube and speedcubing:

- **Casual Solving:** You look at the cube, think about which rule applies, and turn the faces using whole-hand wrist rotations. A solve might take 2 to 5 minutes.
- **Speedsolving:** You recognize piece patterns instantly, execute moves using quick finger flicks without shifting your grip, and plan your next step while turning.

```
CASUAL SOLVING:     Look ────► Think ────► Turn ────► Look ────► Think ────► Turn (2-5 mins)
SPEEDSOLVING:       Recognize ────► Fluid Execution + Lookahead in Parallel (Sub-20s)
```

---

## 2. The Anatomy of a Competitive Solve

Every timed speedcubing solve follows five strict stages:

1. **Scramble:** The cube is mixed using a random 20 to 25-move sequence generated by computer software. This guarantees that every competitor faces a fair, unbiased challenge.
2. **Inspection (15 Seconds):** Before touching the timer, you have up to 15 seconds to pick up the cube, inspect the pieces from all angles, and plan your opening moves. You cannot make any turns during inspection.
3. **Timer Start:** Both hands rest flat on the electronic timer sensors. Lifting your hands starts the clock.
4. **Execution:** You solve the cube as fast as possible using finger tricks and memorized algorithm triggers.
5. **Timer Stop:** The moment the cube is solved, you place both flat hands back down onto the timer sensors to stop the clock.

## 3. Essential Speedcubing Vocabulary

As you practice and explore Cubit, you will encounter these standard terms:

| Term | Full Name | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PB** | Personal Best | Your fastest recorded single solve or average time. |
| **Single** | Single Solve | The time recorded for one individual solve attempt. |
| **Ao5** | Average of 5 | The standard competition metric. You do 5 solves, drop the fastest and slowest, and average the middle 3. |
| **Ao12** | Average of 12 | A standard practice metric. You do 12 solves, drop the best and worst, and average the middle 10. |
| **+2** | Two-Second Penalty | Added to your time if the cube is one turn (>45°) away from solved when stopped. |
| **DNF** | Did Not Finish | Assigned if the puzzle is incomplete or a timing rule was violated. |
| **TPS** | Turns Per Second | The speed of your turning (total moves divided by total seconds). |

## 4. The Milestone Roadmap

Nobody starts by solving a cube in 10 seconds. Every speedcuber progresses through distinct milestones:

```
[ Beginner Method ] ──► [ Sub-60s ] ──► [ Sub-30s ] ──► [ Sub-20s ] ──► [ Sub-10s ]
  Learn to solve         Fingertricks     Intuitive F2L    Full CFOP       World-Class
  layer-by-layer         & basic triggers & 2-Look LL      & Lookahead     Mastery
```

- **First Solve:** Learning the basic layer-by-layer method and finishing the cube without checking notes.
- **Sub-1 Minute:** Developing muscle memory for common move sequences and eliminating long pauses.
- **Sub-30 Seconds:** Transitioning to modern CFOP, solving the first two layers simultaneously (F2L).
- **Sub-15 Seconds:** Mastering full algorithm sets, high turning speeds (6+ TPS), and continuous lookahead.

---

## 5. Key Takeaways

- **Speedcubing is a sport**: Combines pattern recognition, efficiency, and finger dexterity.
- **Standardized timing**: Solves use computer scrambles, a 15-second inspection window, and electronic timers.
- **Averages matter more than singles**: Competitive rankings use **Ao5** (Average of 5) to measure true consistency.
- **Progression is step-by-step**: You start with understanding the cube, learn the beginner method, and gradually build speed.
