With the first layer completed on the bottom, the next goal is solving the **four middle-layer edge pieces**.

When this step is finished, the bottom two layers of the cube—often called **F2L (First Two Layers)** in speedcubing—will be 100% solved.

> [!NOTE]
> Middle-layer edges are pieces that **contain NO yellow stickers** (e.g., Green-Red, Red-Blue, Blue-Orange, Orange-Green).

---

## 1. Finding and Aligning a Middle-Layer Edge

1. Hold the cube with **White on the Bottom (`D`)** and **Yellow on the Top (`U`)**.
2. Scan the top layer for an edge piece that **does NOT have yellow** on either of its two faces.
3. Look at the sticker facing you on that edge (the front color).
4. **Turn the `U` layer** until that front color aligns with its matching side center, creating a vertical **"T" shape** on that face.

```
      [ Y ]           ← Yellow Center on Top
    [ G ] [ G ]       ← Top Edge Front Color matches Center
        [ G ]         ← Creates an upside-down "T" shape
```

---

## 2. Determining Insertion Direction: Right vs. Left

Look at the **top sticker** of the aligned edge piece to see whether it needs to move to the right or to the left:

- If the top color matches the **Right center** $\to$ **Right Insertion**.
- If the top color matches the **Left center** $\to$ **Left Insertion**.

### Case 1: Right Insertion Algorithm
When the edge needs to move into the **Front-Right** slot:

```notation
U R U' R' U' F' U F
```

### Case 2: Left Insertion Algorithm
When the edge needs to move into the **Front-Left** slot:

```notation
U' L' U L U F U' F'
```

<AlgorithmPlayer
  algorithm="U' L' U L U F U' F'"
  title="Left Insertion (U' L' U L U F U' F')"
  description="Moves the top edge away, pairs it with the corner using the left trigger, and inserts it into the front-left slot"
/>

> [!TIP]
> **The Intuitive Pattern**: Notice how both algorithms start by moving the top edge **away** from its destination (`U` for right insert, `U'` for left insert), applying the trigger on the target side, and reconnecting the white pair with the front face.

---

## 3. Handling Trapped or Backwards Edges

What if an edge piece is already in the middle layer, but it is either in the wrong slot or flipped backwards?

1. Hold the cube so the trapped edge is in the **front-right middle slot**.
2. Execute the **Right Insertion algorithm (`U R U' R' U' F' U F`)** once using any dummy yellow edge from the top layer.
3. This pushes the trapped edge out into the top layer.
4. Now align its front color into a "T" and insert it properly!

## 4. Summary & Milestone Check

Repeat this process for all 4 middle-layer edges:
- [x] All 4 middle-layer edges (Green-Red, Red-Blue, Blue-Orange, Orange-Green) are correctly placed.
- [x] The bottom two layers (F2L) are completely solved across all four side faces.
- [x] The white face on the bottom remains completely solved.
