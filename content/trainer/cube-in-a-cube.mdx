The **Cube in a Cube** is widely considered the most visually striking pattern in all of cubing.

By selectively permuting and twisting three adjacent edges and three adjacent corners around a single corner piece, the algorithm creates the optical illusion of a miniature 2x2 cube nestled inside the corner of a full-size 3x3!

> [!NOTE]
> Start with a **fully solved cube**. Hold the cube with White on Top and Green on Front so the nested 2x2 forms in the top-front-right corner.

---

## 1. The 15-Move Cube in a Cube Algorithm

Execute the following 15-move sequence:

```notation
F L F U' R U F2 L2 U' L' B D' B' L2 U
```

## 3. How to Restore the Cube

To return your cube to its solved state, execute the exact **inverse sequence**:

```notation
U' L2 B D B' L U L2 F2 U' R' U F' L' F'
```

## 4. Practice the Sequence

Break the 15-move algorithm into two manageable chunks:
- **Part 1 (Moves 1 to 8)**: `F L F U' R U F2 L2`
- **Part 2 (Moves 9 to 15)**: `U' L' B D' B' L2 U`

## 5. Key Takeaways

- **Optical Illusion**: Constructs a miniature 2x2 cube inside the corner of a 3x3.
- **Algorithm (15 moves)**: `F L F U' R U F2 L2 U' L' B D' B' L2 U`.
- **Restoration**: Execute the inverse sequence `U' L2 B D B' L U L2 F2 U' R' U F' L' F'`.
- **Corner & Edge Coordination**: Demonstrates how algorithms manipulate orientation and permutation simultaneously.
