This is the final physical step of the beginner method.

All 4 corners are already in their correct positions. Now, you will rotate each corner in place until every yellow sticker faces upward—**solving the entire Rubik's Cube**.

> [!CAUTION]
> **The #1 Beginner Trap**: While performing this step, the bottom layers will temporarily look completely scrambled. **DO NOT PANIC.** This is mathematically expected. As long as you complete the full sequence and follow the rules below, the entire cube will restore itself cleanly.

---

## 1. Setup: Holding the Cube

1. **Flip the cube upside down** so the **White face is on Top (`U`)** and the **Yellow face is on the Bottom (`D`)**.
2. Find an unsolved corner on the bottom layer (where the yellow sticker is facing the side or front instead of down).
3. **Turn the `D` layer** until that unsolved corner sits in the **bottom-right position (`DFR`)**.

```
[ White Face on TOP ]        ← Keep White on top throughout this entire step!
[ Target Corner at DFR ]     ← Position unsolved corner in bottom-right
```

---

## 2. The Orientation Sequence: R U R' U'

With the unsolved corner in the bottom-right position, execute the **Right Trigger**:

```notation
R U R' U'
```

Repeat `R U R' U'` **2 times** (or **4 times**) until the yellow sticker of that bottom-right corner faces directly downwards.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Always complete all 4 moves**: Even when you see the yellow sticker drop to the bottom on move 3 (`R U R'`), **you MUST execute the final `U'` turn**! Omitting the `U'` will scramble the rest of the cube.

---

## 3. The Golden Rule: Turn ONLY the Bottom Layer

Once your first corner has its yellow sticker facing down:

> **DO NOT ROTATE THE ENTIRE CUBE.**

1. Keep the same face facing you.
2. **Turn ONLY the bottom layer (`D` or `D'`)** to bring the next unsolved corner into the bottom-right position.
3. Repeat **`R U R' U'`** (2 or 4 times) until this new corner has yellow facing down.
4. Repeat for any remaining twisted corners.
5. When the final corner is oriented, the entire cube will snap into its solved state! Turn the `D` layer once to align the colors if needed.

- [x] All 6 faces are solid colors.
- [x] You have officially solved the 3x3 Rubik's Cube!
