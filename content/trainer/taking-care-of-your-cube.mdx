A modern speedcube is a precision mechanical instrument with dozens of interlocking plastic tracks, tiny springs, and neodymium magnets.

Just like a musical instrument or sports equipment, regular care ensures consistent turning speed, prevents premature plastic wear, and extends the lifespan of your puzzle for thousands of solves.

---

## 1. The Enemies of Speedcube Performance

Three main factors degrade a cube's turning feel over time:

```
[ Dust & Pocket Lint ]  →  Accumulates on internal tracks, creating friction and sludge.
[ Heat & Direct Sunlight ] →  Warps ABS plastic and degrades internal lubricant.
[ Impact & Drops ]      →  Can crack corner stems, dislodge internal magnets, or bend core screws.
```

---

## 2. Daily Habits for Cube Longevity

Simple daily practices make a massive difference in keeping your puzzle in top condition:

1. **Wash Your Hands Before Solving**: Skin oils, snack residues, and microscopic dirt transfer directly to the cube's exterior and work their way into internal tracks.
2. **Avoid Hard Surface Drops**: Practice over a soft desk mat, bed, or carpeted floor. Dropping a cube on tile or asphalt can chip plastic edges or dislodge internal magnets.
3. **Never Carry a Loose Cube in a Pocket or Backpack**: Pockets and backpacks are full of lint and grit. Always store your cube inside a protective fabric pouch or hard shell case.
4. **Keep Away from Extreme Heat**: Never leave your speedcube in a hot car, on a sunny windowsill, or near a radiator. High temperatures can soften ABS plastic, distort tolerances, and dry out lubricants.

---

## 3. Routine Surface Cleaning (Without Disassembly)

You do not need to take your cube apart every week. For regular maintenance every 2 to 3 weeks:

```
[ Step 1: Open an Edge Gap ]
Gently rotate the top layer 45° and pull an edge slightly outward to expose the internal tracks.

[ Step 2: Wipe with Microfiber ]
Use a dry, lint-free microfiber cloth or a soft cotton swab to wipe away visible gray dust and dried lube.

[ Step 3: Turn and Repeat ]
Rotate the faces and repeat for all four quadrants of the cube.
```

> [!WARNING]
> Never rinse your cube under running water or submerge it in a sink. Water gets trapped inside center piece cavities, rusting metal screws, springs, and core hardware!

---

## 4. When Does Your Cube Need a Deep Clean?

Look out for these clear warning signs:

| Symptom | Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scratchy, gritty sound on turns** | Sand, dirt, or hardened plastic dust on contact tracks | Wipe down internal tracks with a microfiber cloth |
| **Sluggish, gummy resistance** | Old, oxidized lubricant mixed with dust | Deep clean pieces and reapply fresh silicone lube |
| **Spring squeaking or clicking** | Dry metal-on-metal friction at the center screw/washer | Apply high-viscosity silicone core lube to the spring |
| **Loose, wobbly layers** | Screws backing out or worn compression settings | Retighten and rebalance tensions across all 6 faces |

---

## 5. Proper Storage Options

To keep your cube clean and ready for practice:

- **Microfiber Drawstring Pouch**: Lightweight, shields against scratches and dust during daily travel.
- **Hard Shell Cube Box**: Recommended when transporting cubes inside a packed backpack or suitcase.
- **Desk Display Stand**: Keeps the cube elevated off dusty tabletops and presents your collection neatly.

---

## 6. Summary Checklist

- [ ] Store your cube in a protective pouch or case when traveling.
- [ ] Practice over a desk mat or soft surface to absorb accidental drops.
- [ ] Wipe internal tracks with a dry microfiber cloth when dust appears.
- [ ] Keep the puzzle away from moisture, direct sunlight, and extreme heat.
- [ ] Transition to lubrication when turning becomes dry or gummy.
