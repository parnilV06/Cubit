In 2-Look OLL, you take two looks and execute two algorithms to orient the last layer.

In **Full OLL**, you recognize the top-layer pattern in a single glance and execute **one algorithm** to turn the entire top face yellow in **0.8 to 1.5 seconds**. This saves an average of **2 to 3 seconds** and 5 to 7 moves per solve.

There are **57 total OLL cases**. Rather than treating them as an intimidating list of 57 formulas, elite speedcubers group them into **geometric case families** based on the shape the yellow stickers form on the top face.

> [!NOTE]
> You do **not** need Full OLL to become sub-20 or even sub-15 seconds. Master Full PLL first (only 21 algorithms). Learn Full OLL gradually at a pace of 2 to 3 algorithms per week.

## 2. Family Breakdown and High-Yield Cases

### Family 1: Cross Cases (OLL 21 to 27) (7 Cases)
All 4 edges are already oriented into a cross. You already know all 7 of these from **2-Look OLL** (Sune, Anti-Sune, H, Pi, U, T, and L).

### Family 2: T-Shapes (OLL 33, 45) (2 Cases)
The yellow stickers form a bold "T" letter across the top face.
- **OLL 45**: `F (R U R' U') F'` (Oriented in 6 moves using Sexy Move)
- **OLL 33**: `(R U R' U') (R' F R F')` (Sexy Move + Sledgehammer!)

### Family 3: Corners Solved / Pure Edge Flips (OLL 28, 57) (2 Cases)
All 4 yellow corners are already facing up, but 2 or 4 edges need orientation.
- **OLL 28 (M-Case)**: `r U R' U' r' R U R U' R'`
- **OLL 57 (H-Edge Flip)**: `(R U R' U') M' (U R U' r')`

### Family 4: W-Shapes (OLL 36, 38) (2 Cases)
Two corners and two edges form a staircase or "W" pattern.
- **OLL 36**: `L' U' L U' L' U L U L F' L' F`
- **OLL 38**: `R U R' U R U' R' U' R' F R F'`

### Family 5: P-Shapes (OLL 31, 32, 43, 44) (4 Cases)
A 2x2 solid yellow block with an adjacent edge forms the letter "P" or "d".
- **OLL 31**: `R' U' F (U R U' R') F' R`
- **OLL 32**: `L U F' (U' L' U L) F L'`

### Family 6: C-Shapes (OLL 34, 46) (2 Cases)
A solid 3-sticker bar on one side with two outer corners forms a "C" bracket.
- **OLL 46**: `R' U' R' F R F' U R`

## 4. The 4-Tier Progressive Learning Roadmap

Do not attempt to memorize all 57 cases alphabetically. Follow this battle-tested 4-Tier progression:

```
[ Tier 1: The Fast Triggers (15 Total Cases) ]
• All 7 Cross cases (OLL 21–27) [From 2-Look OLL]
• 2 T-Shapes (OLL 33, 45) [Uses Sexy Move + Sledgehammer]
• 2 Corners Solved (OLL 28, 57)
• 4 Easy P-Shapes (OLL 31, 32, 43, 44)
→ Covers over 50% of your real solves!

                    ↓ (Practice 2 weeks)

[ Tier 2: C-Shapes, W-Shapes & Easy L-Shapes (12 Cases) ]
• 2 C-Shapes (OLL 34, 46)
• 2 W-Shapes (OLL 36, 38)
• 8 Easy L-Shapes (OLL 47, 48, 49, 50, 53, 54)

                    ↓ (Practice 2 weeks)

[ Tier 3: I-Shapes & Knight Move Shapes (14 Cases) ]
• 4 I-Shapes / Lines (OLL 51, 52, 55, 56)
• 4 Knight Move Shapes (OLL 13, 14, 15, 16)
• 6 Awkward Shapes (OLL 29, 30, 41, 42)

                    ↓ (Final Polish)

[ Tier 4: The 8 Dot Cases (8 Cases) ]
• No edges oriented (OLL 1, 2, 3, 4, 17, 18, 19, 20)
• Rare frequency, but complete your full 57-case library!
```

## 5. Key Takeaways

- **Full OLL saves 2 to 3 seconds**: One look and one algorithm turns the top face yellow.
- **Group by geometric shapes**: Learn by families (T-shapes, W-shapes, P-shapes, etc.) rather than isolated numbers.
- **Prioritize high-frequency cases**: Tier 1 cases (Cross, T, P) cover the vast majority of solves.
- **Master Full PLL first**: 21 PLL algorithms yield higher time savings with far less memorization.
