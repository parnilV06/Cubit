Beyond the classic Checkerboard and Cube in a Cube, the Rubik's Cube offers a rich playground of geometric patterns, optical illusions, and famous mathematical positions.

Here is a curated gallery of the most captivating algorithms to expand your repertoire.

> [!NOTE]
> All patterns below start from a **fully solved cube**. Each pattern includes both its creation algorithm and its exact restoration sequence.

---

## 1. Six Spots (Center Swap)

The **Six Spots** pattern swaps every center piece with the center of an adjacent face, creating a clean single-dot contrast on all six sides while leaving all 8 corners and 12 edges untouched.

### Algorithm (8 moves):
```notation
U D' R L' F B' U D'
```

## 2. The Wire Cross

The **Wire** pattern creates a sharp plus-sign grid of contrasting colors framing every face in just 8 swift turns.

### Algorithm (8 moves):

## 3. Vertical Stripes

The **Vertical Stripes** pattern transforms all four lateral faces into clean two-tone vertical columns.

### Algorithm (17 moves):
```notation
F U F R L2 B D' R D2 L D' B R2 L F U F
```

### How to Restore:
Execute the inverse sequence:
```notation
F' U' F' L' R2 B' D L' D2 R' D B' L2 R' F' U' F'
```

## 4. The Legendary Superflip (God's Number)

In 1995, mathematician Michael Reid proved that the **Superflip** is one of the furthest possible states from solved on a Rubik's Cube. In 2010, a supercomputer proof established that any 3x3 position can be solved in 20 moves or fewer ("God's Number"), with the Superflip serving as the premier 20-move maximum distance state.

In this state:
- All 8 corners are in their **correct position and orientation**.
- All 6 centers are **completely solved**.
- **Every single one of the 12 edges is flipped in place!**

### Algorithm (20 moves):
```notation
U R2 F B R B2 R U2 L B2 R U' D' R2 F R' L B2 U2 F2
```

### How to Restore:
Because the Superflip is **self-inverse**, executing the exact same 20-move algorithm a second time returns the cube to 100% solved!

---

## 5. Key Takeaways

- **Six Spots (`U D' R L' F B' U D'`)**: 8-move center swap.
- **Wire Cross (`R L U D' F B' R L`)**: 8-move perimeter framing.
- **Superflip (20 moves)**: The premier 20-move God's Number state where all 12 edges are flipped in place.
- **Reversibility**: Every pattern can be returned to solved using its inverse sequence or by repeating self-inverse algorithms.
