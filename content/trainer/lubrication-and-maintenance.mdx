Lubrication is the single most effective way to customize the speed, smoothness, and control of your speedcube.

A properly lubricated cube turns with minimal effort, protects contact surfaces against microscopic plastic abrasions, and eliminates annoying spring squeaks.

---

## 1. Why Do Speedcubes Need Lubricant?

When dry ABS plastic pieces slide against each other at high speeds, friction causes micro-scratches, uneven wear, and a chalky, resistant turning feel.

Specialized cube lubricants serve three vital functions:
- **Friction Reduction**: Creates a thin liquid film that lets pieces glide smoothly across each other.
- **Speed & Control Tuning**: Allows you to speed up a stiff puzzle or slow down an uncontrollable, overly fast one.
- **Hardware Protection**: Cushions internal tracks, prolonging the lifespan of pieces and cores.

---

## 2. Speedcube Lubricant Categories

Cube lubricants are categorized primarily by **viscosity** (liquid thickness measured in centistokes, cSt).

```
[ Low Viscosity (Fast / Light) ]   →  100 to 1,000 cSt   (e.g., Lubest Pro, Lunar, Weight 1)
[ Medium Viscosity (Balanced) ]    →  1,000 to 5,000 cSt (e.g., Martian, Silk, Weight 3)
[ High Viscosity (Control / Core) ]→  10,000 to 50,000 cSt (e.g., Weight 5, Gravitas, Nebula)
```

| Type | Viscosity | Common Examples | Primary Effect | Where to Apply |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fast / Speed** | Light | Lubest Pro, QiYi M-Lube, Lunar | Maximizes speed, reduces friction | Piece contact tracks |
| **Balanced / Smooth** | Medium | MoYu Lube V1, Martian, Weight 3 | Provides plush, smooth, controllable glide | Piece contact tracks |
| **Control / Gummy** | Heavy | Weight 5, Angstrom Gravitas | Slows down overshooting, adds tactile drag | Corner feet and internal tracks |
| **Core / Spring** | Ultra-Heavy | Heavy Silicone 50k cSt, Weight 5 | Eliminates metal spring squeaks and clicks | Springs, washers, and core screws |

> [!CAUTION]
> **Dangerous Substances to NEVER Use**:
> - **WD-40 or Spray Lubricants**: Contain petroleum solvents and propellants that melt ABS plastic within minutes!
> - **Cooking / Vegetable Oils**: Go rancid, rot, and turn into a sticky, foul-smelling varnish.
> - **Petroleum Jelly (Vaseline)**: Softens and chemically degrades puzzle plastic.
> - **Hand Lotions or Baby Oils**: Contain water, perfumes, and emulsifiers that rust metal springs.
> 
> Only use dedicated silicone or synthetic lubricants manufactured specifically for twisty puzzles.

## 3. Step-by-Step: How to Lubricate Piece Tracks

You do not need to disassemble your cube to apply piece lubricant.

```
[ Step 1: Clean First ]
Wipe away any visible dust or dried residue from the outer layers using a microfiber cloth.

[ Step 2: Open an Edge Gap ]
Turn the top face 45° so an edge piece sits halfway between two corners. Gently pull the edge outward to expose the internal sliding tracks.

[ Step 3: Apply 1 to 2 Drops ]
Dispense 1 to 2 small drops of piece lubricant directly onto the contact track of the internal piece stem.

[ Step 4: Break in with 20 to 30 Solves ]
Turn all faces rapidly or complete several solves. The turning action spreads the lubricant evenly across all 26 pieces.

[ Step 5: Test & Assess ]
Evaluate the turning speed. If the cube still feels dry, add one more drop. If it feels sluggish, turn through another 20 solves to complete break-in.
```

> [!TIP]
> **The Golden Rule of Lubing**: Less is more! It is easy to add an extra drop, but removing excess lubricant requires wiping down every internal piece by hand.

## 4. How to Silence Squeaky Springs (Core Lubrication)

If your cube makes a metallic clicking or squeaking noise when turning a face, metal-on-metal friction between the screw head, washer, and spring is the culprit.

To silence it:
1. Pop off the center cap of the squeaky face using a small fingernail or plastic spudger.
2. Place **one tiny drop of heavy silicone lube (Weight 5)** directly on top of the screw head so it seeps down into the spring coils and washer.
3. Replace the center cap and turn the face 15 to 20 times. The squeak will disappear instantly.

*(Note: If your cube uses a **MagLev** repulsion system instead of metal springs, core squeaking is impossible, so spring lubrication is not required!)*

---

## 5. Cleaning Out Old Lubricant (Deep Cleaning)

Every 3 to 6 months (or whenever your cube feels sticky and gummy), perform a deep reset:

1. **Disassemble the Cube**: Turn the top layer 45°, pop out one edge piece with gentle upward thumb pressure, and remove all remaining edges and corners.
2. **Wipe Every Piece**: Use a dry microfiber cloth or paper towel to wipe old lubricant, lint, and grime off every piece stem and contact face.
3. **Wipe the Core**: Clean the internal core assembly without loosening the screws.
4. **Reassemble**: Assemble the bottom cross, then first-layer corners and second-layer edges, and finally the top layer.
5. **Re-lube Fresh**: Add 1 to 2 drops of fresh lubricant and break it in.

---

## 6. Key Takeaways

- **Silicone is King**: Use 100% silicone puzzle lubricants (Light for speed, Heavy for control and springs).
- **Start Small**: 1 to 2 drops is plenty for an entire 3x3 cube.
- **Never Use Household Oils**: WD-40, cooking oils, and Vaseline will destroy your puzzle.
- **Clean Before Re-lubing**: Adding fresh lube on top of dusty old residue creates abrasive sludge.
