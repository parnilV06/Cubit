In the previous lesson, you learned that single letters like `R` and `U` mean turning a face 90 degrees clockwise.

Now let's look at the two modifiers that complete the basic notation system: **Prime (`'`)** and **Double (`2`)** turns.

---

## 1. Prime Turns (Counter-Clockwise)

An apostrophe or prime symbol (**`'`**) indicates a **90-degree counter-clockwise turn** (opposite to the standard move).

- **`R'` (R-Prime)**: Right face counter-clockwise (moves downwards).
- **`L'` (L-Prime)**: Left face counter-clockwise (moves upwards).
- **`U'` (U-Prime)**: Top face counter-clockwise (moves to the right).
- **`D'` (D-Prime)**: Bottom face counter-clockwise (moves to the left).
- **`F'` (F-Prime)**: Front face counter-clockwise.
- **`B'` (B-Prime)**: Back face counter-clockwise.

## 2. Double Turns (180 Degrees)

A number **`2`** after a face letter means turning that face **180 degrees** (a half turn).

- **`R2`**: Turn the Right face 180 degrees.
- **`U2`**: Turn the Top face 180 degrees.
- **`F2`**: Turn the Front face 180 degrees.

> [!TIP]
> Because 180 degrees in either direction produces the exact same piece placement, `R2` and `R2'` result in the identical cube state!
