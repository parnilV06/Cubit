With your White Cross completed and aligned on the bottom, the next goal is solving the **four white corners**.

When this step is complete, the entire white face will be solved, and the bottom row of every side face will form a solid matching colored band.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A corner piece has **3 colors**. A corner is only solved if its white sticker faces the bottom AND its two side stickers match their corresponding side centers.

---

## 1. How to Identify the Target Corner and Slot

1. Hold the cube with the **White Cross on the Bottom (`D`)** and the **Yellow center on the Top (`U`)**.
2. Look at the top layer for any corner piece containing a **White sticker**.
3. Identify the corner's other two colors (for example, **White-Green-Red**).
4. Find the slot on the bottom layer located between the matching centers (between Green and Red).
5. **Turn the `U` layer** until that corner is positioned directly **above its target slot** in the **top-right position (`UFR`)**.

```
[ Target Corner in Top-Right (UFR) ]  →  White-Green-Red Corner
                 ↓
[ Target Slot in Bottom-Right (DFR) ] →  Between White, Green, and Red Centers
```

---

## 2. The Insertion Algorithm: The Right Trigger

To insert and twist the corner into its target slot, you use the fundamental 4-move sequence known as the **Right Trigger** (or *Sexy Move*):

```notation
R U R' U'
```

Watch how the Right Trigger moves the corner piece into position while preserving the solved white cross:

### How Many Times to Repeat:
With the target corner in the top-right position directly above its destination slot, repeat `R U R' U'` until the corner drops into place with its white sticker facing down:

- **White facing Right**: Execute `R U R' U'` **1 time**.
- **White facing Top**: Execute `R U R' U'` **3 times**.
- **White facing Front**: Execute `R U R' U'` **5 times**.

> [!TIP]
> Always execute all 4 moves of the trigger! A very common beginner mistake is stopping after the 3rd move (`R U R'`) because the corner looks solved. **You must always complete the final `U'`**, or your cross will remain broken.

---

## 3. Handling Trapped or Flipped Corners

What if a white corner is already in the bottom layer, but it is either in the wrong slot or flipped with white facing the side?

1. Hold the cube so the trapped corner is in the **bottom-right position (`DFR`)**.
2. Execute **`R U R' U'` once**.
3. This kicks the corner out into the top layer without disturbing any other solved pieces.
4. Now proceed with standard insertion by matching its target slot!

## 4. Summary & Checklist

Repeat this process for all four white corners until the first layer is 100% complete:
- [x] The entire white face on the bottom is solid white.
- [x] The bottom layer on the Green face is a solid row of 3 green stickers.
- [x] The bottom layer on the Red face is a solid row of 3 red stickers.
- [x] The bottom layer on the Blue face is a solid row of 3 blue stickers.
- [x] The bottom layer on the Orange face is a solid row of 3 orange stickers.
