In the fourth and final stage of CFOP, your goal is **PLL (Permutation of the Last Layer)**: rearrange the last-layer corners and edges into their solved positions to complete the puzzle.

Full PLL contains **21 unique cases**. While learning 21 algorithms is completely achievable, **2-Look PLL** lets you complete 100% of your CFOP solves right now using **only 6 algorithms**!

```
[ Solid Yellow Top Face (Completed OLL) ]
                   ↓
[ Stage 1: Corner Permutation (CP) ]
Put all 4 corners into their correct positions (2 algorithms: T-Perm or Y-Perm)
                   ↓
[ Stage 2: Edge Permutation (EP) ]
Cycle the remaining edges into place (4 algorithms: Ua, Ub, H, or Z)
                   ↓
[ SOLVED RUBIK'S CUBE! ]
```

> [!NOTE]
> **Orientation vs. Permutation**: OLL turns all yellow stickers facing up. PLL moves the pieces horizontally around the perimeter without changing their orientation.

## 1. Stage 1: Corner Permutation (2 Cases)

Scan the four sides of the top layer for **Headlights** (two corners on the same face sharing the same side color):

```
       [ Top View: Searching for Headlights ]
           ┌──────────┐
  Orange ──│ █  ░░  █ │── Orange (Headlights found!)
           │ ░░ ░░ ░░ │
           │ █  ░░  █ │
           └──────────┘
```

### Case 1A: Headlights Found (Adjacent Corner Swap)
If you see one pair of headlights:
1. Place the headlights on the **Left** side of the cube.
2. Execute the **T-Permutation**:

<AlgorithmPlayer
  moves="R U R' U' R' F R2 U' R' U' R U R' F'"
  speed={1}
  title="Stage 1: T-Permutation (Adjacent Swap)"
  description="Swaps the two right-hand corners into place while preserving bottom layers"
/>

### Case 1B: No Headlights (Diagonal Corner Swap)
If no side has matching corner stickers, two diagonally opposite corners must be swapped:
1. Hold the cube from any angle.
2. Execute the **Y-Permutation**: `F R U' R' U' R U R' F' R U R' U' R' F R F'`

*Result of Stage 1: All four corners are now completely solved!*

## 2. Stage 2: Edge Permutation (4 Cases)

With all four corners solved, look at the four edges around the perimeter:

### Case 2A: Ua-Perm (3 Edges Counter-Clockwise)
- **Recognition**: 1 full solid bar (solved face) in the **Back**. The remaining 3 edges need to cycle **counter-clockwise** (Front edge goes to Left).
- **Algorithm**: `R U' R U R U R U' R' U' R2`

### Case 2B: Ub-Perm (3 Edges Clockwise)
- **Recognition**: 1 full solid bar (solved face) in the **Back**. The remaining 3 edges need to cycle **clockwise** (Front edge goes to Right).
- **Algorithm**: `R2 U R U R' U' R' U' R' U R'`

### Case 2C: H-Perm (Opposite Edges Swap)
- **Recognition**: No solved face. Opposite colors need to swap across the center (Front swaps with Back; Left swaps with Right).
- **Algorithm**: `M2 U M2 U2 M2 U M2`

### Case 2D: Z-Perm (Adjacent Edges Swap)
- **Recognition**: No solved face. Adjacent edges need to swap (Front swaps with Right; Back swaps with Left).
- **Algorithm**: `M2 U M2 U M' U2 M2 U2 M'`

## 5. Key Takeaways

- **PLL completes the solve**: Rearranges the 8 last-layer pieces into their solved slots.
- **2-Look uses only 6 algorithms**: 2 for corners (T & Y), 4 for edges (Ua, Ub, H, Z).
- **Find headlights first**: Headlights on Left $\to$ T-Perm; No headlights $\to$ Y-Perm.
- **Cycle edges to finish**: Check for a solved back bar (U-perms) or cross-swaps (H/Z perms).
