When solving a Rubik's Cube, you often need to look at a different side of the puzzle.

Instead of describing moves from an awkward side angle, speedcubers use **whole-cube rotations**. These moves reorient the entire cube in your hands without turning any individual layer.

> [!NOTE]
> Whole-cube rotations are written in lowercase: **`x`**, **`y`**, and **`z`**. They correspond to the three spatial axes in 3D geometry.

---

## 1. Face Turns vs. Whole-Cube Rotations

There is a fundamental difference between face turns and rotations:

- **Face Turn (`R`, `U`, `F`)**: Turns a single layer. This changes the relative positions of pieces and scrambles or solves the puzzle.
- **Cube Rotation (`x`, `y`, `z`)**: Rotates the entire physical puzzle. No pieces move relative to each other; only your viewpoint changes.

```
FACE TURN (R):          Turns only the right 3x3 layer (permutes pieces)
CUBE ROTATION (x):      Spins the ENTIRE cube upwards (changes perspective)
```

---

## 2. The Three Rotation Axes: x, y, and z

Each rotation letter corresponds to turning the entire cube in the direction of a specific standard face:

| Rotation | Axis | Follows Face | Physical Movement | Result (with Green Front, White Top) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`x`** | X-Axis (Horizontal) | **`R`** | Rotates the cube **upwards** (away from you) | Green Front moves to **Up**; Yellow Bottom moves to **Front** |
| **`y`** | Y-Axis (Vertical) | **`U`** | Rotates the cube **to the left** | Red Right moves to **Front**; Green Front moves to **Left** |
| **`z`** | Z-Axis (Depth) | **`F`** | Tilts the cube **clockwise** (rolls to the right) | White Top moves to **Right**; Orange Left moves to **Up** |

### Prime and Double Rotations:
Just like face turns, rotations can use prime (`'`) and double (`2`) modifiers:

- **`x'` / `y'` / `z'`**: 90° counter-clockwise rotation along that axis (e.g., `x'` follows `R'`, rotating the cube downwards).
- **`x2` / `y2` / `z2`**: 180° half rotation along that axis (e.g., `y2` turns the cube around to look at the back face).

### The 360° Reset Rule:
Rotating four quarter turns in the same direction brings the cube back to its exact starting orientation:

```
x + x + x + x = Starting Orientation
y + y + y + y = Starting Orientation
z + z + z + z = Starting Orientation
```

## 5. Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Confusing `x` and `y`**: Remember that `x` follows the `R` face (pitching up/down), while `y` follows the `U` face (yawing left/right).
- **Confusing Face Turns with Rotations**: An uppercase `R` turns the right layer; a lowercase `x` rotates the entire cube.
- **Over-Rotating During Solves**: In speedcubing, rotating too frequently wastes time. Modern methods prioritize minimizing rotations through lookahead and back-slot insertions.

---

## 6. Summary & Key Takeaways

- **`x`**: Rotates the entire cube upwards around the X axis (follows `R`).
- **`y`**: Rotates the entire cube horizontally around the Y axis (follows `U`).
- **`z`**: Tilts the entire cube clockwise around the Z axis (follows `F`).
- **Piece states are unchanged**: Rotations only change your perspective, not piece permutation.
- **Modifiers apply**: `x'`, `y'`, and `z'` are 90° counter-clockwise; `x2`, `y2`, and `z2` are 180°.
