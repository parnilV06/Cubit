In 2-Look PLL, you take two looks and execute two algorithms.

In **Full PLL**, you instantly recognize the case from any angle and execute **one algorithm** to solve the entire cube in **0.8 to 1.8 seconds**.

Full PLL contains exactly **21 algorithms**. Because every single solve ends with PLL, learning Full PLL is the single highest-return algorithm investment you can make in your speedcubing journey.

> [!NOTE]
> You already know **6 of the 21 algorithms** from 2-Look PLL (T-Perm, Y-Perm, Ua, Ub, H, and Z). That means you only have **15 new algorithms** left to achieve 100% Full PLL mastery!

## 5. AUF: Adjust Upper Face

When your PLL algorithm finishes, the cube is completely solved, except the top layer might be offset by 90° or 180°.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Never rotate the whole cube (`y` / `y'`) to align the solved layer.**
> Instead, finish the solve with a single index or middle finger flick: `U`, `U'`, or `U2`. This final adjustment is called **AUF (Adjust Upper Face)**.

## 7. Key Takeaways

- **Full PLL saves 2 to 3 seconds per solve**: One look, one algorithm to finish.
- **You only need to learn 15 new algorithms**: 6 were already learned in 2-Look PLL.
- **Recognize with Bars and Headlights**: Scan for 1x3 bars, 1x2 blocks, and matching headlights.
- **Finish with AUF**: Use `U`, `U'`, or `U2` to align the final layer with zero cube rotations.
