## 1. Master Algorithm Cheat Sheet

Here are all the algorithms used in the beginner method in one unified table:

| Step | Goal | Algorithm | Notes / Mnemonic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. White Cross** | Plunge matched edge to bottom | **`F2`** (or `R2`, `L2`, `B2`) | Match side color with center first |
| **2. First Layer** | Insert corner into bottom-right | **`R U R' U'`** | Repeat 1x, 3x, or 5x |
| **3. Middle Layer (Right)** | Insert top edge to Front-Right | **`U R U' R' U' F' U F`** | Push away $\to$ Right trigger $\to$ Connect |
| **3. Middle Layer (Left)** | Insert top edge to Front-Left | **`U' L' U L U F U' F'`** | Push away $\to$ Left trigger $\to$ Connect |
| **4. Yellow Cross** | Orient top yellow edges | **`F R U R' U' F'`** | Dot $\to$ L-shape (12 & 9) $\to$ Horizontal Line |
| **5. Yellow Edges** | Align side colors of cross | **`R U R' U R U2 R' U`** | Sune: Hold matched edges at Back & Right |
| **6. Position Corners** | Cycle top corners into slots | **`U R U' L' U R' U' L`** | Niklas: Hold correct corner in Front-Right |
| **7. Orient Corners** | Twist final corners in place | **`R U R' U'`** (2x or 4x) | White on Top; turn **ONLY `D`** between corners! |

---

## 2. The 7-Step Solve Walkthrough

```
[ Step 1: White Cross ]
Gather 4 white edges on yellow center (Daisy) → Align side colors → Turn 180° (F2)

[ Step 2: First-Layer Corners ]
Place corner above target slot (UFR) → Execute Right Trigger (R U R' U') until white faces down

[ Step 3: Middle-Layer Edges ]
Match top edge to front center ("T" shape) → Use Right Insert or Left Insert

[ Step 4: Yellow Cross ]
Identify Dot, L-Shape, or Line → Apply F (R U R' U') F' until cross forms

[ Step 5: Align Yellow Edges ]
Match 2 edges at Back and Right → Execute Sune: R U R' U R U2 R' U

[ Step 6: Position Yellow Corners ]
Place 1 correct corner at Front-Right → Execute Niklas: U R U' L' U R' U' L

[ Step 7: Orient Yellow Corners ]
Flip cube (White Top, Yellow Bottom) → Unsolved corner at DFR → R U R' U' (2x/4x) → Turn D → Repeat!
```

## 4. How to Practice for Speed and Confidence

1. **Practice Step-by-Step**: If you get stuck on Step 4 or 5, don't worry about speed. Solve up to that step several times until recognition becomes immediate.
2. **Wean Off the Cheat Sheet**: Try solving without looking at this sheet, checking it only if you forget an algorithm.
3. **Use the Cubit Timer**: Head over to the **Timer** section to record your first official timed solves!
